SKAPA CREATIVE · BRAND FILES v1
================================

COLOURS
  Blue    #4B4AE4
  Pink    #FF2791
  Yellow  #FFF1A7
  Cream   #EFEEEA
  Ink     #0E0E0E

TYPE
  Signature   Corinthia Bold (already outlined in every file, no font needed)
  Small text  IBM Plex Mono, Medium, wide letter spacing, capitals
  Body text   IBM Plex Sans
  All three are free on Google Fonts.

FOLDERS
  svg/            Vector files. Use these anywhere you can: web, print, Figma, Canva Pro.
                  All text is outlined, backgrounds are transparent (except svg/icon).
    signature/      The Skapa signature. The main logo.
    s-mark/         The S on its own.
    icon/           The S on a square of colour. App icons, profile pictures.
    badge/          The dotted wavy badge with the S.
    oval-seal/      "Skapa . Swedish · To create, to make" seal.
    name-location/  "Skapa Creative · Ipswich . UK" line.
  png/transparent/ The same pieces as transparent PNGs, 2000px on the long side.
  png/social/
    profile-pictures/  1080 and 400px squares. Social apps crop them to a circle; the S sits safely inside.
    banners/           LinkedIn, X, Facebook cover and link-preview (Open Graph) images in blue, cream and ink.
  favicon/         Drop every file into the website's public folder and paste head-snippet.html into <head>.
                   In Next.js: put favicon.ico, favicon.svg and apple-touch-icon.png in /app or /public.

COLOUR PAIRINGS THAT WORK
  Cream on Blue · Pink on Ink · Blue on Cream · Ink on Pink · Blue on Yellow
  Use one brand colour per piece. Use the S or the seal in a corner, not both.

DON'T
  Stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, or re-type the signature in another script font.
  Use the badge or seal below about 60px wide; use the S instead.
